Our Commitment

A fortress for your data

From the moment you sign up, every piece of information you share with RenyK is protected by multiple layers of security.

Data Encryption
All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS (Transport Layer Security). Your sensitive information, like passwords, is hashed to ensure it can never be read by anyone.
Secure M-Pesa & Card Payments
We partner with trusted, PCI-compliant payment gateways to process all transactions. Your financial details are never stored on our servers, ensuring the highest level of security for every payment.
Secure Infrastructure
Our servers are hosted in secure, state-of-the-art data centers with 24/7 monitoring, firewalls, and regular security audits to protect against unauthorized access and potential threats.
Privacy and Data Protection
We are committed to protecting your privacy. We will never share your personal information with third parties without your consent. Our policies are designed to comply with data protection regulations.

Have a security question?

Our team is here to help. Reach out to us if you have any concerns about your data and privacy.
