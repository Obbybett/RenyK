Our Mission: To Simplify Renting in Kenya

We're a passionate team dedicated to building technology that makes life easier for landlords and tenants.

Our Story

RenyK was born from a simple idea: renting should be simpler. Our founders, having experienced the challenges of the Kenyan rental market firsthand—from chasing payments to dealing with delayed repairs—knew there had to be a better way. They envisioned a single platform that could bridge the gap between landlords and tenants, fostering transparency, communication, and efficiency.

We started in a small office in Nairobi with a big goal: to leverage technology to solve real-world problems. Today, RenyK is proud to serve a growing community of property owners and renters across the country, helping them save time, reduce stress, and build better rental relationships.
