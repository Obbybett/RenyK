Our Story

Born from Experience

RenyK was founded by a team of tenants and landlords who were tired of the endless paperwork, late-night calls, and payment-tracking headaches. We knew there had to be a better way. We set out to create a simple, powerful platform that makes the rental process smoother and more transparent for everyone involved.
